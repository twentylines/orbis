# Orbis v0.1.0-beta.91

- Launch apps directly from Home in resizable Orbis windows, with maximize, retained app sessions, and account or per-app opening preferences.
- Browse installed apps in a split preview while keeping your app list, filters and scroll position.
- Set up Orbis with animated onboarding and coordinated sign-in screens, matching theme wallpapers, and appearance, performance and app opening choices.
- Sign in with a username or email, or use a passkey with optional extra authenticator verification. Password sign-in keeps its two-factor settings.
- Choose automatic Orbis updates that wait until the dashboard is unused, and optional daily schedules for individual apps. Automatic updates are disabled by default.
- Read feature overviews and nested changelogs for skipped updates in the update centre.
- Downgrade to compatible Orbis releases from beta 90 onward, with database and Runtipi checks, verified backups, and automatic recovery.
