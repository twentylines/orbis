# Orbis beta 44

## Device history in Monitor

- Record individual Linux interface and whole-device disk readings every ten seconds, independently of browser viewers. Select a device and five-minute, one-hour, 24-hour or seven-day range without opening dozens of charts.
- Show Download/Upload or Read/Write/Active time with measured averages, peaks and sample counts. Keep current readings separate from saved history; missing samples and changed devices break the series instead of becoming zeroes.
- Keep one bounded collector, at most 64 retained device names and at most 720 returned points. Use a separate private journal with minute, five-minute and 30-minute tiers, seven-day retention, append checkpoints and periodic compaction. Report storage failures, unavailable groups and evicted devices explicitly.
- Preserve device/range in the URL and link directly from current host readings. Pause hidden/minimized browser polling, retain the selected device as the catalogue grows and preserve native focus and reading position through refreshes and pending selections.

Upgrade the restricted host service from a reviewed checkout to supply opaque device-generation identities. Older helpers retain current readings and explicitly report missing recordable identities. A core image update does not replace the installed helper. Raw boot IDs, MAC addresses and hardware serial numbers are not returned. Layered disks and virtual interfaces remain separate; disk active time is an approximate kernel reading, not a claim of drive saturation. See [monitoring details](https://github.com/twentylines/orbis/blob/main/MONITORING.md).

## Chart space and recovery

- Move compact chart scales above a full-width plot. Keep graph space and label padding comfortable at 320/390px and doubled rendered text.
- Keep the device selector mounted while new values load, without presenting the previous device's readings under the new name.
- Retain the surviving Retry button after another failed attempt in all three host cards. Focus the heading after recovery and avoid taking focus from another control.
- Retain translucent palette blur on system bars, coloured titlebars and the dock, without bright white frame rims.
