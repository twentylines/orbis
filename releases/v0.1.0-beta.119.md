# Orbis 0.1.0 beta 119

- Dismiss the split-screen app selector as soon as a snapped window is dragged away, with a continuous closing transition instead of waiting for mouse release.
- Restore the selector when a drag is canceled, preserve the app window and its contents, and keep closing selectors out of keyboard and pointer interaction.

Runtipi remains at 4.10.1. This update does not introduce a data migration.
