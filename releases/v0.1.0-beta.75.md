# Orbis beta75

## Appearance

- Choose a custom accent colour for buttons and links, with colours adjusted to keep text readable.
- Adjust panel opacity, bar and dock opacity, blur and tint across windows, menus and notifications.
- Follow system motion and transparency preferences, reduce movement, or use solid surfaces. Low effects disables blur, shadows and decorative motion.
- Save appearance settings per account and apply cached colours before the workspace loads.

## Interface

- Keep selected dock items readable on hover over bright wallpapers.
- Use the same accent and hover colours for app actions and dialog buttons. Native sliders follow light and dark mode.
- Keep focused form controls and their focus rings visible when scrolling, resizing or enlarging text. Preserve input drafts during those changes.
- Update the light and dark appearance screenshots.
