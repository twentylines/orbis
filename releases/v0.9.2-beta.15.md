## Update reliability

- Keep coordinated updates out of older update catalogues that cannot apply them.
- Keep progress tied to the same operation while Orbis and its host updater restart.
- Complete and verify an interrupted backup before restoring the original controller.
- Publish app metadata safely when installed apps and Orbis state use separate mounts.

An enrolled host updater and Sentinel are required. Older installations can use the beta.14 bridge before applying this release from Updates.
