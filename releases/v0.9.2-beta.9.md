## Runtipi compatibility

- Move to the Runtipi 4.12 base with a backed update. Existing installations should first install and verify Orbis 0.9.2 beta 8, which updates the recovery worker.
- Keep the app data, proxy and installed component checks in the coordinated update. The database and queue use a dedicated internal network.

## Recovery

- Continue showing the same update in Orbis and Sentinel during service replacement and recovery. Recovery can reconnect the progress endpoints after the original worker is confirmed stopped.
