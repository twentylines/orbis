# Orbis beta 45

## Keep device history current

- Expire successful host-device snapshots nine seconds after a request starts. This leaves room before the ten-second history tick, and socket latency cannot extend the cache deadline past that tick.
- Keep concurrent readers on one in-flight request and retain the two-second retry delay after an unavailable result finishes. The helper still performs at most one bounded collection every ten seconds.
- Retain beta44's per-device disk/network history, measured averages/peaks/counts, private journal, device/range links and full-width compact charts.
- Retain the translucent palette blur on the top bar, coloured desktop titlebars and dock, without white frame rims.

The real beta44 no-browser gate found mostly 20-second samples and delayed/unavailable history. The previous completed-response cache overlapped the collector timer. Its reviewed OTA and independent snapshot/app/store/preference preservation checks passed; its background-history gate was rejected. [Actual rejected measurements](https://github.com/twentylines/orbis/blob/main/screenshots/host-device-history/rejected-real-background.json).

## Verification boundary

The source regression uses the actual host-telemetry service and history timer with deferred responses and previous helper-generation timestamps. Both 100ms and nine-second response cases reject the old cache and pass the corrected deadline across 130 seconds without browser reads. Request-start expiry, shared requests and unavailable/rejected completion backoff also pass. Backend types pass.

The frontend is unchanged from the separately verified device-history source build. Desktop/tablet/phone, doubled rendered text, twelve palette/mode combinations, device/range selection, delayed/error states and native inspection retain their [source evidence](https://github.com/twentylines/orbis/blob/main/screenshots/host-device-history/README.md).

The corrected published background collector, private journal, exact partial-bucket restart continuity and real browser rendering remain acceptance gates until recorded in [DEPLOYMENT.md](https://github.com/twentylines/orbis/blob/main/DEPLOYMENT.md). A core update does not replace the restricted helper; the reviewed helper with device-generation identities remains required.

This is a development prerelease. Sensors/GPU, full theme packs, multiple homelabs and complete product/real-data migration acceptance remain open. Hades is unchanged.
