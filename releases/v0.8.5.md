# Orbis 0.8.5

- Redesigned Home with editable application layouts, saved widget arrangements and clearer system information.
- Improved animated wallpapers, glass surfaces, light and dark themes, and wallpaper-aware sign-in and sign-out text.
- Unified workspace loading and sign-in transitions around the Orbis logo.
- Aligned Settings with the other workspace pages and made Enter open the first search result.
- Fixed Settings category selection waiting on background requests, and deferred backup modules until they are needed.
- Added automatic Sentinel status refresh and independent component update support.
- Updated the modular command-line installer and Orbis graphics.
- Strengthened backup preparation, archive validation and recovery safeguards.

Time Machine remains experimental. Full restore and standalone recovery workflows are still being completed.
