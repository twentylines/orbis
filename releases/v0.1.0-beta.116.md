# Orbis 0.1.0 beta 116

- Align the system bar's labels and controls, with consistent themed focus fills instead of perimeter rings.
- Open connection and window-layout menus below the click position, within the app frame. Reload keeps connection options open, and paired actions share the same size.
- Bring background app windows forward when clicked without reloading their pages, and blend minimising windows into their tray icons.
- Use the same Orbis time editor for account schedules, individual apps and onboarding. Invalid times stay editable and cannot silently submit an earlier value.
- Replace the remaining browser dropdown in app-opening preferences and show whether the wallpaper follows the colour preset or stays fixed.

Runtipi remains at 4.10.1. This update does not introduce a data migration.
