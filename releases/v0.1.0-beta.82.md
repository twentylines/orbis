# Orbis v0.1.0-beta.82

- Apps now supports alphabetical, recently opened and recently installed sorting, category filters and clearer visibility for new installations.
- Reduced redundant app-event refreshes, hidden-tab activity and animation layout work while preserving the glass effects and appearance.
- Startup and sign-in share one continuous loading scene. Fixed dock clipping and unwanted window borders while minimizing apps; Home activity cards keep their expanded layout.
- Clearer system update controls, independent bar/dock blur settings and sliders with live previews.
