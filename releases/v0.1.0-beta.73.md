# Orbis beta73

## Appearance

- Upload a private PNG, JPEG or WebP wallpaper for your account. Replace it, return to a bundled wallpaper, or remove the upload from Settings → Appearance.
- Adjust image fit, horizontal and vertical position, dimming and blur. Save these settings with your account and restore the image layout independently.
- Keep workspace labels readable over uploaded images, fade between wallpapers and fall back to a bundled image if an upload is unavailable.
- Preserve the selected wallpaper when an upload is interrupted. Importing settings or restoring defaults cancels an unfinished upload.
- Keep uploaded images out of settings exports and signed-out screens. Images are limited to 8 MB and 32 megapixels.
- Give image controls visible keyboard focus and room to reflow with enlarged text.
- Use “Server” as the initial name for new accounts; retain existing saved server names.

curl -fsSL https://twentylines.github.io/orbis/install | sudo python3
