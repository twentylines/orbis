# Orbis beta 24

## Themes and UI wording

- Replace the default notifications with Orbis cards. Notifications follow the selected colour preset and light/dark mode, support dismissal and keyboard focus, and keep long stacks within the screen.
- Keep open dialogs in place when dismissing a notification. Resume notification timers after a hovered stack is emptied, and wrap notification actions on narrow screens.
- Apply shared theme colours to authentication, guest access, loading/error screens, alerts, tooltips, controls, and Compose editors.
- Replace browser confirmation prompts inside the Compose editor with an Orbis confirmation dialog. Cancelling preserves the draft; stale confirmations cannot overwrite newer changes.
- Correct narrow guest-header and editor-footer layouts, error detail contrast, and dark-mode links.
- Use practical English labels and instructions throughout the dashboard, search, appearance, authentication, tasks, updates, backups, and notifications. Remove repeated headings and filler introductions. Preserve configuration identifiers, operational warnings, and upstream attribution.

Runtipi compatibility remains 4.10.1. Full product acceptance and the separate Hades deployment phase remain open.
