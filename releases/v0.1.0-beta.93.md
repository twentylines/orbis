# Orbis v0.1.0-beta.93

- Add a Get started card to fresh Home screens, opening the Store directly. It stays out of the way after apps or previous activity are detected.
- Apply a colour preset to buttons and the Orbis logo by clearing the previous custom accent when a preset is selected.
- Prevent downgrades to beta 90 from erasing newer app-opening and wallpaper settings. Orbis checks every account before review and again before applying; compatible settings can still use the existing downgrade path.
