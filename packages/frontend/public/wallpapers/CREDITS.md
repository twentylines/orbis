# Wallpaper photos

Main images are 3840 × 2160 WebP, except Ocean at its native 3529 × 1985 crop. Picker previews are 480 × 270 or 640 × 360 WebP. Each photo is bundled under the listed source license.

### Retained immutable theme-pack images

These three original files remain available under their original Unsplash License
so the bundled v1 theme-pack manifests continue to resolve to the exact bytes they
identify. They are not used as the six wallpaper preset defaults.

- `theme-pack-alpine-v1.webp` — Willian Justen de Vasconcellos, [Tre Cime di Lavaredo](https://unsplash.com/photos/grey-mountain-during-daytime-0HGKG23yMew), Unsplash License.
- `theme-pack-tideline-v1.webp` — Sean Oulashin, [Oahu shoreline](https://unsplash.com/photos/seashore-during-golden-hour-KMn4VEeEPR8), Unsplash License.
- `theme-pack-woodland-v1.webp` — Ali Kazal, [Misty pines in Quebec](https://unsplash.com/photos/green-pine-trees-on-foggy-weather-7b39feeIMO0), Unsplash License.

The Forest, Rose and Ocean v3 photographs are cropped to a desktop aspect ratio and encoded as WebP without changing their colours. Earlier v2 images remain available for existing saved references.
